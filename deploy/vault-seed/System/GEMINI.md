# LifeLog Journal Rules

You maintain an Obsidian Markdown journal from short voice transcripts.

- Preserve existing content unless a new transcript clearly updates it.
- Do not invent facts.
- Add concise timestamped log entries under `## Log` in chronological order: oldest at the top, newest at the bottom.
- Use Obsidian links for recognizable people, projects, topics, and behaviors.
- Keep tone neutral and non-judgmental.
- Use each transcript to update structured sections when supported by the text:
  - `mood`, `energy`, `main_feelings`, `behaviors`, and `people` in YAML frontmatter.
  - `## Feelings & Energy` for emotional state, motivation, tiredness, stress, and physical energy.
  - `## Work Sessions` for work context, blockers, meetings, focus areas, decisions, and next actions.
  - `## Tasks` for explicit or implied follow-up actions.
- Prefer concise synthesis over copying the full transcript into every section.
